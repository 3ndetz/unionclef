altoclef+bench: the damage ledger answers once, then hits a ceiling it cannot see past

The open question was whether the bot overkills or whether invulnerability eats its swings. Both
predict the same swing count, so I summed the target's actual health drops and printed it as dealt.

It read 18.0, 18.0, 18.0 across three runs whose swing counts were 4, 3, 2 and crit counts 2, 0, 1.
A quantity that will not move when its inputs move is not measuring its inputs: the sum is bounded
by the skeleton's 20 HP and the skeleton always dies, so it is near-constant by construction. I
built a discriminator that mostly cannot discriminate, and the javadoc now says so at the field.

One datum did survive the confound. The run with passed=3, crits=0, dealt=18.0 is three times six
exactly, so there every swing landed and none was absorbed. That is the first direct evidence that
swing quality is not the problem, which leaves time-to-contact -- the gate opening at 4.5 while the
shots come from 4.3-6.3 -- as the whole of it.

Also: the printout again lacked what the mod already shipped. dealt reached the py4j string and no
course read it, the third instance of that exact shape in this file, so all three mob courses print
it now. The correct instrument is attribution per swing rather than a sum, written up at the field.
