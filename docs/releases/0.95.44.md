# 0.95.44 — endermen from a pillar

Endermen were most of the nether deaths. They are now fought from the top of a three-block pillar.

## Fixed

- **Endermen.** 1.21 melee lands only when the attacker's box — its hitbox widened sideways, not
  upwards — touches the target. An enderman is 2.9 tall, so from the ground it cannot reach feet
  three blocks up, while the player reaches its head from about 1.8 blocks. The pearl hunt picks a
  site with ground all round and nothing an enderman could stand on level with the top, clears a
  plant off it, pillars, provokes by looking, looks at the feet of an angry one (vanilla freezes a
  stared-at enderman), swings when it is in reach and heals on top. An angry one that cannot get to
  the pillar gets a new pillar next to it; a pillar with nothing in range is left for one nearer the
  endermen. A 3x3 roof at +2 was tried first: real placement cannot build it from the ground.
- **The pillar was stopped every 0.7 s** by the orphan-route guard, which only knew about drives.
  A task that starts a route itself now claims it.

## Bench

- **Playthroughs ran at 10 fps.** gamer_smoke never lifted vanilla's idle cap for an unfocused
  client (run_suite always did). Now 24-30.
- New course `mob_endermen_shelter` (hurt, blocks in the pack, the real pearl hunt via
  `@test huntender`); it turns natural regeneration on, which another course leaves off.

## Validation

- Nether from rung-ender, 30 min: n51 no deaths, health full throughout, 9/14 pearls. Before this
  work the same 30 minutes gave 1 to 7 deaths.
- mob_endermen_shelter 4/4 (damage 0, 0, 11.3, 0), and PASS again on the release build.
- nav 17/17. mob: melee, weapon_swap, unarmed, endermen, endermen_hurt PASS; mob_trio and
  mob_skeleton fail as they did on the control before this work.

## Known

- Fewer pearls per minute than the open fight: sites are strict in a warped forest.
- Deaths after a respawn in the Overworld (zombie, drowning, falls) are not addressed here.

## How to test

```
python3 deploy/runner/run_suite.py mob --only mob_endermen_shelter --repeat 3
python3 deploy/runner/gamer_smoke.py 30 --from rung-ender --record
```
