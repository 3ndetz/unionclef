# A stopped bridge stays on the bridge

Stopping a bridge (`;stop`, `stopPathing()`, or any task that cancels navigation) now
releases MOVE_BACK as well. The bridge walks backwards while it places (the backplace),
and its stop never released that key: the bot let go of sneak, kept walking backwards and
fell off its own bridge. Measured on 0.95.58: one stop at 2.4 s, then y -60 -> -248 and
"fell out of the world". The bug is older than 0.95.58; the 0.95.58 tests found it.

**HOW TO TEST:** on a flat floor with cobblestone in the hotbar, face open air and start `;bridge 30`
(or Py4J `bridgeForward("east", 30)`), wait two seconds, then `;stop`. The bot must stand
still at the edge; no key stays pressed.

Measured on the release candidate (bridge fix only, over 0.95.58):

- Bridge and pillar stops: 6/6 pass (three each). All keys released, aim cleared, the body
  stays on the floor (drift under 0.04 blocks), 30 FPS, health 20. The six recordings were
  reviewed.
- Stop while MovementQueue is placing across a walled 6-block gap: 3/3 pass, 29-30 FPS.
- 15-minute native playthrough from `full59-before-portal`: PASS, 0 deaths, median 30 FPS,
  iron ore at 47.5 s and iron at 117.7 s. The recording was reviewed. About ten of those
  minutes are spent in a dirt shelter: the shelter treats a daytime thunderstorm as night
  (`WorldHelper.canSleep`). That is older behaviour and is tracked as a separate item.

No speed or FPS gain is claimed. The long full59 lava stall and the whole playthrough are
still open.

python deploy/runner/gamer_smoke.py 15 --from full59-before-portal --raw-resume --record
