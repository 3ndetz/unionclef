# 0.32.0

Ranged combat: the bot's bow aim now runs on tungsten's vanilla-physics
ballistics instead of the old approximation, and leads moving players.

## Bow aim via TrajectorySolver

`ShootArrowSimpleProjectileTask` (altoclef's combat shoot task, used by `@shoot`
and by `KillPlayerTask` at range) used a closed-form pitch estimate with
`g=0.006` and a hand-tuned lead multiplier. Direct fire now calls tungsten's
`TrajectorySolver`, which simulates the real arrow arc (drag 0.99, gravity 0.05,
speed 3.0/tick, pitch solved by bisection over simulated flights).

Three things had to line up for it to actually hit:

- **Aim for the full-draw arc.** Recomputing the charge from the draw time every
  tick swung the ballistic pitch ~18° during the draw, and it only settled on the
  release tick — so the aim was always a step behind and missed. Aiming for the
  full-draw arc (as the tungsten `BowShooter` primitive already did) keeps the
  pitch target stable, so it converges well before release. This alone took a
  stationary target from 2/5 to 5/5 at 24 blocks.
- **Lead from self-tracked position, not `getVelocity()`.** A normally-walking
  remote player reports ~0 `getVelocity()` on the client (only knockback sets it),
  so leading off it never worked. The task now differences the target's position
  each tick (light EMA) and feeds that to the solver.
- Weapon selection and the decision of *when* to shoot stay on the altoclef side
  (`KillPlayerTask`: melee under 10 blocks, bow/ender-pearl at range). Only the
  aim moved. High-angle artillery (no line of sight) keeps the closed-form lob,
  which is also the fallback when there's no flat solution (out of arrow range).

## New primitive: `targetKinematics(name)`

py4j/agent helper — a named player's client-side position, `getVelocity()` and
`onGround`. Handy for aim/lead decisions (with the caveat above that
`getVelocity()` is ~0 for a walking player, so difference the position yourself).

## Testing

`deploy/runner/bow_altoclef_test.py` fires `@shoot` at a standing target at 24
blocks (full charge) and gates on that — **5/5**, which validates the aim. A
`targetKinematics` diagnostic confirmed the lead velocity reaches the solver
(`getVelocity()` is populated when a target genuinely moves, and the aim uses it).
A moving-target volley runs too but is informational: the stand can't produce a
smooth server-side moving target (a bot `;goto` sprint-*jumps* — airborne, erratic
client lerp bursts no ballistic lead can predict; a held key doesn't sustain a
walk), and that's not how a real running player moves. The tungsten-primitive path
(`bow_test.py`, py4j `shootArrowAt`) is unchanged.

## Known / in progress

- Vanilla arrow spread is real: even a perfect full-charge shot at 24 blocks is
  ~3-4/5 on a stationary target by design.
- MLG bow-in-jump (shooting mid-parkour with player inertia) waits on the
  tungsten parkour physics gap (2-block walls / gap jumps in the executor).
- Full-game mob survival is a separate track.
