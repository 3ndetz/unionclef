# 0.32.0

Ranged combat: the bot's bow aim now runs on tungsten's vanilla-physics
ballistics instead of the old approximation.

## Bow aim via TrajectorySolver

`ShootArrowSimpleProjectileTask` (altoclef's combat shoot task, used by
`@shoot` and by `KillPlayerTask` at range) used a closed-form pitch estimate
with `g=0.006` and a hand-tuned lead multiplier. Direct fire now calls
tungsten's `TrajectorySolver`, which simulates the real arrow arc (drag 0.99,
gravity 0.05, speed 3.0/tick, pitch solved by bisection over simulated flights).

Target lead comes from the target's **per-tick position delta**, not
`getVelocity()`: other players are moved by position packets, so their
client-side velocity reads ~0 and the solver would never lead a running target.
Feeding the position delta gives it the real motion.

Weapon selection and the decision of *when* to shoot stay on the altoclef side
(`KillPlayerTask`: melee under 10 blocks, bow/ender-pearl at range) — only the
aim moved. High-angle artillery (shooting over obstacles with no line of sight)
keeps the closed-form lob, which also serves as the fallback when no flat
solution exists (target out of arrow range).

## Testing

- `deploy/runner/bow_altoclef_test.py` — fires `@shoot` at a standing then a
  running victim at ~18 blocks. Pass: >=3/5 standing, >=2/5 running (vanilla
  arrow spread makes perfect accuracy impossible by design).
- The tungsten-primitive path (`bow_test.py`, py4j `shootArrowAt`) is unchanged.
