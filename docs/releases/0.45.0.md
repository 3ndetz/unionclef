# 0.45.0

Fixes the stuck SHIFT / sneak key. **Combat is NOT fixed in this build** — it is being
re-done as a full rework (see below); do not expect working PvP from 0.45.0.

## Stuck SHIFT / sneak fix

Sneak (SHIFT) could stick pressed for several seconds — most visibly after pressing sprint
near a ledge. Cause: the void-guard and the combat edge-protection press sneak near a rim to
stop you sliding off, but never released it; when the driving task (flee / punk / combat)
ended, the pressed sneak was left over your own control (`resetAllState` only releases keys
on disconnect, not on task end).

Fix: the mod now releases the keys it forces (sneak / attack / use) once, on the moment it
stops driving — leaving your WASD / sprint / jump alone so your own held movement isn't
touched. The void-guard also releases its edge-sneak as soon as you're clear of the rim.

Please verify live (this one is hard to reproduce on the test stand).

## Combat — honest status (NOT fixed here)

Live testing showed PvP / punk / follow are **not** working, and the earlier "done" marks on
them were wrong (they passed the stand test but that does not equal live). Re-opened. Root
diagnosis so far:

- **Doesn't move to a moving target / doesn't approach when close** — the immediate movers are
  off by default (`followBlockPathFinderEnabled`, `enableLeap`), so follow depends on the slow
  physics pathfinder which re-plans forever on a moving target and stands still.
- **Doesn't click to attack even with a clear line** — the attack gate itself is correct; it
  doesn't fire because the bot isn't in reach (no approach) and the aim shakes (never within
  the angle window).
- **Aim shakes** — the camera chases a position-packet target that jumps every tick, with no
  smoothing.

Combat is a full rework (reliable approach at all ranges + stable aim + always-moving bunny-
hop/strafe + blocking-entity handling), tracked as a dedicated pass in `TODOS.md`. Not shipped
here on purpose — shipping a half-fix is what caused the false "done" in the first place.
