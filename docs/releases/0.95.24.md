# 0.95.24 — the flood stops deadlocking on a lava lake it is stuck under (G108, underground)

Follow-up to 0.95.23. Closes a hard deadlock in the overworld obsidian flood when the bot reaches
the nether-portal stage DEEP UNDERGROUND (where a real playthrough usually is, having just mined
diamonds) rather than at the surface.

## The stall

From the `nether-reach` checkpoint (bot at y=37 in a mine) the `@gamer` playthrough descended to a
lava lake at y=21, ended in a cramped air pocket at (788,19,819) directly UNDER the lake's edge,
committed to a rim ON the lake it could not climb up to, and FROZE there for the entire run --
obsidian 0, no portal, no nether. Confirmed frozen at the same block from t=696s to t=1668s
(~27 minutes), `mqSteps=0`, `dbTick=0`, `UnstuckChain` owning the bot.

## The root: optimistic reachability + a shimmy that fakes progress

Two independent defects combined into a permanent hang:

1. **`rimFor` accepted a rim it could not click.** It kept a rim if EITHER the rim OR the water cell
   above it was reachable (`canReach(rim) || canReach(rim.up())`). But water is placed by clicking
   the rim's TOP face, and `InteractWithBlockTask` marks the RIM (not `rim.up()`) unreachable when it
   cannot get there. `canReach` is just `!isUnreachable`, so `rim.up()` stayed optimistically
   reachable and the flood re-selected the same unreachable rim forever.

2. **Both progress guards measured raw body displacement.** When the flood then stalled, the
   `UnstuckChain` shimmied the wedged body in place. The flood's `MovementProgressChecker` and
   `InteractWithBlockTask`'s move-checker both read the jiggle as movement and reset every tick, so
   neither ever concluded the rim was unreachable and blacklisted it. A deadlock built entirely out
   of "the body moved."

`canReach`'s optimism is the same trap 0.95.23 called out for BURIED lava (a source with no air
above); this is its horizontal twin -- a lava lake with air above whose rim the body still cannot
reach, because it is standing under the lake.

## The fix (isolated to `PlaceObsidianFloodTask`)

- **The clickable rim itself must be reachable** (`canReach(rim)`), so the unreachable-mark that
  `InteractWithBlockTask` already sets actually removes the rim from selection.
- **The rim commit is guarded by APPROACH, not displacement.** Distance to the water cell only falls
  when the body is genuinely getting to the rim, so the shimmy's in-place jiggle cannot fake it. The
  flood blacklists a rim after ~10 s of the body never getting closer, then re-selects; with the
  reachable-looking rims exhausted it explores for lava it can actually stand beside. It rests once
  the body is within placing range, so it cannot punish a normal surface approach.

Both changes are confined to the flood. The surface flood (flat bench, and the natural-terrain
surface build validated in 0.95.23) approaches a reachable rim, the distance falls, the guard never
trips -- so it is a no-op there and cannot regress it.

## Validation

- Deterministic repro captured as checkpoint `flood-stuck` (bot frozen at 788,19,819 under the lake).
- Fix run from `flood-stuck`: **deadlock broken.** The bot left the frozen block within ~1 minute of
  `@gamer` starting (before this fix it sat there for 27 minutes), blacklisted the unreachable rim,
  and relocated -- (788,19,819) -> (796,16,779) -> (799,24,770), ascending out of the pocket toward
  accessible terrain, with `UnstuckChain` ownership back to 0 (it was 1919 while wedged).
- Regression, flat obsidian flood (OBS bench): **PASS** -- `NETHER_PORTAL LIT at (2358,-56,358)` in
  273 s, real portal block confirmed. The flood made all 17 obsidian and built+lit the frame exactly
  as before; the approach guard rests in range on the flat pad, so it is a verified no-op there.
- Regression, nav baselines (`nav_flat`, `nav_staircase`, `nav_descend`): **3/3 PASS** (~29 fps, 0
  freezes, 0 self-falls, 0 invalid runs) -- the change is confined to the obsidian flood and does not
  touch navigation.
