# 0.95.43 — out of lava, and food in the nether

From a 45-minute nether run (13/14 pearls, then out of food and dead) and its checkpoint,
`nether-nofood`, resumed exactly as it was frozen.

## Fixed

- **The lava escape stood still.** Three defects stacked: every planner refused lava while escaping
  (the port had dropped baritone's `canSwimThroughLava`, MovementHelper:166), so the escape fell to
  the physics search, which took 11 s to answer with the body burning; the escape's goal was the
  nearest cell without lava, which is the air over the bot's head; and with nothing to place, the
  place branch took every tick from the drive. Surface lava is now swum while escaping, the goal
  needs a floor, and in lava the block planner takes over at once.
- **The physics route hugged lava.** Its block guide paid no margin for a cell beside lava (the other
  planners pay twelve walks), and that executor tolerates 2.6 blocks of drift. Same price now.
- **No food in the nether** meant wandering forever. The food search hunts hoglins, counts raw meat at
  raw value where nothing can be cooked, and goes back to the Overworld when no food is in sight.

## Bench

- New course `escape_lava_pool`: a 7x7 sheet of lava, the bot in the middle.
- No `rung-*` checkpoints after the run has died (a respawn overwrote rung-iron-tools with a bare one).

## Validation

- escape_lava_pool 0/2 -> 3/3 (+2/2), escape_lava 0/2 -> 3/3 (+2/2).
- nav 17/17 twice (nav_gaps once on the retry: its documented void-jump flake; no lava in it).
- nether-nofood, 20 min: hoglins, portal to the Overworld, pigs, a smoker, 20/20 health, no deaths.
  Before: a lava death at minute 5 on the same state.

## Known

- 13 minutes at 1.3 hearts underground in the Overworld before the first food was found.
- Enderman fights in the nether's terrain; water escape without tools.
