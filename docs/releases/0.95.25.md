# 0.95.25 — the obsidian collect refuses to mine obsidian that sits against lava

Follow-up to 0.95.24. A defensive step against the underground lava deaths, plus the honest scope of
what it does and does not cover.

## What it fixes

When the flood turns a lava lake to obsidian, it converts the SURFACE sources the water reaches, but
lava remains deeper in the basin. The obsidian collect then followed the vein DOWN into the basin and
the body stepped onto the exposed lava -- measured from the deep nether-reach checkpoint: obsidian
0->5 while y fell 21 -> 20.3, then "tried to swim in lava" and a respawn ~950 blocks away.

`CollectObsidianTask` now treats an obsidian block as a target only when none of its six neighbours is
lava, so the bot never has to stand against lava to mine it, and it passes the same gate to
`MineAndCollectTask` (a new OPTIONAL, null-by-default target filter, so the 37 other callers are
byte-for-byte unchanged). When only lava-adjacent obsidian is left, the collect comes back empty and
the flood runs another cycle, converting more of the basin before mining resumes. Drops on the ground
are always collected (a dropped item needs no safe stand).

## What it does NOT fix (open, next pass)

The underground lava death has a SECOND mode this release does not address. From the `flood-stuck`
death-trap (bot wedged in a pocket UNDER the lake), the bot committed to an in-range rim beside the
lava and "burned to death" while flooding -- BEFORE it ever reached obsidian mining. Both modes share
one root: in the cramped space around an underground lake the pathfinder steps the body into lava.
The real fix is robust lava-avoidance in cramped quarters (a tungsten pass), and it is deliberately
left for a focused, carefully-regression-tested change rather than rushed in here.

So this release removes one death trigger (mining into the basin) and is a no-op everywhere lava is
not adjacent to the obsidian; it does not by itself make the deep-underground portal build survivable.

## Validation

- Regression, flat obsidian flood (OBS bench): **PASS** -- `NETHER_PORTAL LIT at (2358,-56,358)` in
  216 s, real portal block. On the flat lake all lava converts, so every obsidian is lava-safe and the
  filter is a no-op -- confirmed it does not starve the surface build.
- Underground (`flood-stuck`): the mining-into-lava death mode is gated out, but the bot still
  "burned to death" flooding from under the lake (mode two, above) -- so the underground build is not
  yet survivable. Documented as the next focused pass.

## How to test

```
OBS=1 python3 deploy/runner/nether_portal_test.py                              # flat: still builds + lights
python3 deploy/runner/gamer_smoke.py 20 --from flood-stuck --daylock --no-save-end  # underground: mode-two burn remains
```
