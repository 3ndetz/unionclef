# 0.95.49 — food before diamonds, a bed that gets placed

## Fixed

- **Out of food, still mining.** With no food source in sight (underground none is tracked) food
  collection had priority 0.1 at any reserve, below every ore. full52 mined for five minutes with
  nothing to eat, hunger 11 and no regeneration, then met a skeleton, a zombie and a spider at 15
  health and died. Under 20 food units (two or three items) collection now has priority 100.
- **A whole night on "Placing a bed nearby".** The bed task cleared a 3x2x3 box and laid a 3x3
  platform at the nearest site whose cells were merely air or breakable (mid-air included). It now
  works on the strip the bed uses (stand, foot, head, headroom, floor) and picks the site by
  distance plus eight blocks per dig or fill. On the same checkpoint the bot now places the bed
  and sleeps within a minute. A bed at hand also gets 90 s a night; after that the night is
  waited out as without one.
- **Night shelter on open flat ground** could not close its roof: the cap went into the ground-
  level cell, with the dug hole under it and air beside it. It now digs three deep and caps one
  below the surface, where the ground itself is the wall.
- **The last nether portals survive a checkpoint resume** (py4j getLastNetherPortals /
  setLastNetherPortal, stored in the checkpoint meta by gamer_smoke).
- Bench: the death watch had been off since 0.95.47 (a string added to an int, swallowed); fixed,
  and it now says so if it breaks again.

## Validation

- night_shelter_flat (new, open 7x7 pad): 6/7 PASS; the failing run never started (the task saw
  the old day time; the course now waits for it).
- full52 checkpoint five minutes before its death: food collection at priority 100 at once; bed
  placed and slept in at dusk.
- mob suite 8/10: trio and unarmed fail as on earlier builds.

## Known

- Searching for food with none in sight is a random wander: on the same resume it spent 90 s
  stuck in a gap and walked into a cave, where a zombie killed the bot at 10 health.
