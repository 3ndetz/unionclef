# 0.95.35 — the free-form VoidGuard stops vetoing the walker's planned descents (chase_terrain)

`chase_terrain` had failed on every build measured this cycle, the 0.95.29 release included (A/B on
the same stand, the jar inside both containers verified). It was about to be filed as
"pre-existing" and left. Instrumented instead:

```
punkStats    inactive=0 noTarget=0 for the first ~30 s        the chase WAS on, target in hand
walkMode     491 off / 3542 BFS / 0 direct                     the walker was driving
walkerHeldAbove   3541 of those 3542 BFS ticks                 held over a waypoint below
guide        (-289,117,289) -> (-290,114,290)                  a planned 3-block diagonal drop
body         yaw 44.8 vs bearing 45.0, forwardStealers none    facing it, pressing forward
corners      air / leaf_litter, drop column open, lands on grass    nothing in the way
```

The walker pressed forward toward a legitimate drop and the body did not move. The reason was
written in the player mixin, two paragraphs above the guard, about MovementFall: at the lip of a
PLANNED three-block drop, `VoidGuard` measures fallHeight 4 one step ahead against its hardcoded
maxSafeFall of 3, releases forward and forces sneak, and vanilla ledge protection pins the body.
The queue was exempted from that by `!movementOwnsTick`, and the note claimed the exemption also
"keeps the guard off ... the walker". It did not: `movementOwnsTick` is `MovementQueue.isRunning()`
and nothing else, so under `PunkPlayerTask` the guard ran after the walker every tick.

The guard is for FREE-FORM movement. The walker in BFS mode follows a PLANNED route whose descents
are deliberate and whose hazards `RouteHazards` (0.95.34) re-checks every tick, so the guard now
stands aside while `BlockPathWalker.isFollowingRoute()`. DIRECT -- free-form steering at a point --
keeps it. Counter: placeStats `vgYieldRoute=N`.
