# 0.95.48 — eat, or fight, not both

## Fixed

- **Bite, mob steps in, run, bite again.** The food chain checked for enemies with
  `getCloseEntities()`, which is only what a sword can reach (about 3.5 blocks), so its 7-14 block
  danger ranges never saw anything further away. Every bite began with a mob "absent" and was
  broken off as it stepped into reach. The ranges were also the wrong way round (7 to begin, 14 to
  keep going). Now: begin a bite with no hostile within 7 blocks and no angry hostile that sees us
  within 10; break it off only when one is within 3.5.
- **Running from a mob it can beat.** With no armour and under 18 health (always, for a hungry bot
  that does not regenerate) any zombie within 8 blocks sent the bot running, ahead of the fight
  branch that rates the same zombie beatable. Over 10 health, with the mobs in range beatable by
  the fight branch's own score, the bot now fights (creepers excepted).
- The agent tool `getEntitiesInfo` read the same reach-only list; it now lists every tracked
  entity (`EntityTracker.getAllTrackedEntities`).

## Validation

- New course mob_hungry (sword, food, 14 health, empty stomach, one zombie from 10 blocks).
  0.95.47: 10 and 23 bites begun for one meal, 40 s of running. Now 5/5: one bite each, no damage,
  zombie dead, food 6 -> 20.
- mob suite 7/9: trio (3 damage) and unarmed fail as on earlier builds; skeleton passed on the retry.
- pvp suite 10/12: narrow_bridge_duel and allround fail as on earlier builds (allround 7:8 on
  09-23, 6:7 on 08-14); melee_basic and chase_terrain passed on the retry.
- getEntitiesInfo lists a cow at 8 blocks and sheep at 40-75 (before: nothing past 3.5).
