# A reachable hole for the night

Night shelter searches loaded terrain above and below the body and routes to any
safe destination through the ordinary Tungsten move graph. It refreshes completed
approaches when the terrain changes, and checks the destination again before the
first dig. Flood, lava, wall and break-policy checks remain in force.

HOW TO TEST: resume a saved water entry with blocks in the original inventory, run
`@test shelter`, and watch the bot leave the pool, dig a dry three-cell shaft and
cap it. `night_shelter_new_site` exercises a newly available destination;
`night_shelter_blocked_nearest` exercises an obsolete destination. General idle rescue
must stay off for those isolated controls. A successful hold remains sealed for
ten seconds with no lost health.

Planner requests capture player position, bounding box, ground/water/climbing state
and available building blocks on the client thread before starting the worker.
Condition routes retain their caller identity, including shelter-to-Flee handoffs.
Live-world reads, mining-tool state and the shared placement budget still need
their separate thread-safety work.

HOW TO TEST: run the Flee and shelter ownership controls, plus flat, stair,
descent, water and mixed-tool navigation. A gateway planning request must capture
its player inputs on the client thread and preserve cancellation.

Validation: the exact candidate passed51 arena checks and six fresh saved-entry
holds from `cp1002-0229-t656`, with default pathing, original inventory, sampled
health20, no deaths or lava entries and clean planning/cleanup gates. All captures
were reviewed. A matched published0.95.54 control selected no site during90s
(average27.8FPS); nearby cells passed the unchanged safety predicate.

Known limits: the raw15min gamer replay sheltered and resumed in the morning,
but acquired no new food or rung. Its water-to-bank food pursuit also stalled in
a separate5min published0.95.54 replay (median27FPS); this input remains open.
Repeated approach hops near a shelter rim, the original lava-column stall and
the complete game remain open. This release does not claim a full playthrough.
