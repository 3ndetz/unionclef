## 0.66.0 — mobs fight on tungsten, and crafting stops needing the recipe book

All three benches green at once, which is what this release is for:

| bench | result |
|---|---|
| `run_suite.py nav` | **12/12**, gate failures 0 |
| `run_suite.py mob` | **PASS** (new suite) |
| `gamer_smoke.py 8 --repeat 3 --rung wood` | **3/3**, zero deaths |

### Mob combat runs on tungsten

Mobs used to be fought by hand-rolled code while PvP had the good engine. They now go through
tungsten's `CombatController` — aim at a predicted position, hold a striking distance,
circle-strafe, time crit jumps, break contact below half a bar.

Two things had to be fixed before any of it could execute:

- **weapon damage was hardcoded to zero on 1.21.11** (a stub with a TODO, because the old API was
  removed). The "can I deal with this?" sum therefore came out zero against a zombie's one, so the
  answer was NEVER yes and the bot fled every mob — with an iron sword, at full health. Damage now
  comes from the item's own attribute modifiers.
- the **force field** — the part that swings while the bot is busy doing something else — now
  strikes the nearest hostile with tungsten's trigger bot. It still touches no movement keys, so
  the bot keeps chopping wood while swatting.

Try it: `python deploy/runner/run_suite.py mob`, or in game stand near a zombie with a sword.

### Crafting no longer depends on the recipe book

`useCraftingBookToCraft` now defaults to **false**: the book is disabled on plenty of servers and
a bot that relies on it simply stops there. Crafting places the items in the grid instead.

That change cost a regression on the way (3/3 → 1/3) and the cause was worth finding: the
materials check looks at the INVENTORY, while manual crafting puts ingredients into the GRID, so
any interruption stranded them there and the bot collected materials for ever while its planks sat
in front of it (measured: 8051 collect ticks out of 8051, and the fix fires 646–1827 times a run).
The craft now takes its own ingredients back out of the grid first.

### Dying no longer abandons the goal

A task ends on "finished OR stopped", and the second half fires on any interruption — a death
being the ordinary one. The runner was then switched off entirely and the bot stood where it
respawned for the rest of the run. An interrupted goal is now re-armed (bounded to three
attempts). Reproduce: `@goto` something far, `/kill` the bot mid-way, watch it carry on.

### Stalls

Three separate mechanisms closed, each measured rather than assumed:

- a **rewind wiped the step clock**, so a step that could never finish never timed out — the
  no-progress clock now lives on the queue and measures the body;
- the **forward skip did not reset movements**, and a sticky SUCCESS meant the chain was consumed
  in place;
- **planning that never becomes a route** no longer holds the tick.

### Known

- The stall is a family, not one bug. Captures still show signatures none of these cover.
- `nav_slime` is fps-sensitive; on a loaded machine the suite marks it INVALID rather than failed.
- Manual crafting is slower than the recipe book where the book works — it is the portable choice,
  not the fast one.
