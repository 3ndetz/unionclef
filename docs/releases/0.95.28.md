# 0.95.28 — lava below counts as the void: the nether lava death was knockback off a ledge

Follow-up to 0.95.27. Closes (on its measured mechanism) the death that ended every nether-stage run
so far: "tried to swim in lava" while hunting Endermen.

## What the instrument said

Three earlier clamps on the bot's OWN movement (0.95.25's obsidian filter, 0.95.26's flee veto and
VoidGuard key-release -- the latter reverted for freezing the bot) did not stop it, and a 3-second
bench tracer never caught the instant. So this release first adds a lava-entry INSTRUMENT
(`WorldSurvivalChain`, py4j `lavaEntryStats()`): a snapshot of the body on the rising edge of
`player.isInLava()`. From `nether-fresh`, on the tick the bot entered lava:

```
hurtTime=10  onGround=0  horizontalSpeed=0  flee=0  combatFwd=0  stage=NARROW_BATTLE
```

A hit had just landed, the body was airborne and not walking, forward was NOT pressed, and combat
was in NARROW_BATTLE. An Enderman hit knocked the bot off a narrow ledge into the lava below. Not a
step of its own -- which is why clamping its steps changed nothing.

## The fix (one check in one primitive)

`VoidDetector.fallHeight` scans down for a collision shape. Lava has none, so the scan fell straight
through a lava pool to the stone beneath: a two-block pool read as a three-block drop, "safe", and
nothing in the void machinery engaged at a lava edge. Now a lava block in the column returns
`MAX_SCAN_DEPTH` -- lava below is the void. Every existing, tested void behaviour then applies to
lava edges unchanged: `edgeAhead` / `VoidGuard`'s DIRECTION-SPECIFIC steering veto (only the input
pointing INTO the drop is cancelled -- 0.95.26's blanket-release lesson honoured), sneak at the edge
(vanilla edge protection against being pushed off), `NARROW_BATTLE` detection and repositioning
away from the edge, "toward-island input kept under knockback", and `safeFleePoint` refusing edge
candidates. The pathfinder does not consult `VoidDetector`, so pathfinder-driven work beside lava
(the obsidian flood placing water, obsidian mining) is unaffected.

## Second fix, found while validating the first: no "escape to the surface" under a roof

The first `nether-fresh` run on this build died 100 s in -- `doomed to fall by Ghast` -- before any
lava was near. The chain: the bot started enclosed (a 1x1 netherrack hole), `TimeoutWanderTask`
handed the body to `PlannedEscape`, whose second goal choice is "the surface above" via
`getTopY(MOTION_BLOCKING)`. In the nether that is the bedrock ROOF: `Planned escape (wander
enclosed) -> 108.5,128.0,99.5`, then y=79 at 60 s, y=117 at 82 s, a fireball, a fall, and an
empty respawn at world spawn. `PlannedEscape` now skips the surface step when
`world.getDimension().hasCeiling()`; a roofed dimension's escape is the nearest standable cell,
which the build engine digs to sideways.

Why the bot was enclosed at the start is a harness defect, also fixed: the nether-entry hook in
`gamer_smoke.py` treated "in the nether" as "entered the nether" and re-saved `nether-fresh` over
itself one poll into every `--from nether-fresh` run, so the checkpoint drifted to wherever the bot
was a minute in. Entry is a transition now (overworld -> nether); the checkpoint is re-captured by
a natural run from `nether-reach`.
