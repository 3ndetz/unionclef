# 0.31.0

More tungsten-primary navigation robustness on top of the 0.30.x terrain fix.

## Invalid goals no longer stall the bot

A goal cell that isn't standable can never be reached exactly, so the search
used to stall at it. Two common cases are now snapped to the nearest standable
cell so the bot actually approaches:

- **Goal inside a solid block** → stand on top of it.
- **Goal floating in the air** → drop to the ground below. (A click on a grass
  block reports the cell *above* the surface, so `@goto`/agent clicks landed a
  block too high and the bot just stood there.)

Valid, already-standable goals are returned unchanged — normal navigation is
untouched (swap/staircase regression PASS). Verified: `@goto` to an air cell 5
blocks above the floor walks the bot to the ground beneath it.

## Parkour fallback

When the drift-immune walker stalls (a move it can't do — a gap jump or a
2-block wall), it now hands that segment to the physics executor for a window
instead of spinning, then re-tries the walker. The executor still can't do those
parkour moves itself (that's a deeper block-space gap, tracked), but the handoff
means the walker can't get trapped on a parkour waypoint.

## Known / in progress

- Parkour move generation (gap jumps, 2-block-wall climbs) is still missing from
  the tungsten block-space search — normal walking/climbing terrain is what's
  covered.
- Ranged/MLG (bow in combat) and full-game mob survival are separate tracks.
