# 0.41.0

Two things: the bot can bridge across a gap it can't jump, and a goal it can't stand on
(air, tall grass) no longer makes it compute forever.

## Bridge-as-a-move (#46)

`@goto` used to give up at the edge of a wide gap: parkour (v0.40.0) clears gaps up to
4 blocks, but anything wider had no move and the bot just stalled and abandoned the
goal. Now, when nav can't otherwise make progress and the bot is standing at the edge
of a real gap (cell ahead clear, no floor for 2+ blocks down) with the goal across it
at roughly the same level and a block in the inventory, it **paves a bridge toward the
goal** (the godbridge primitive) and walks across — the second half of place-as-a-move
(pillar-up was the first).

- Pillar when the goal is overhead, bridge when it's across a gap (mutually exclusive).
- `equipBuildBlock` auto-equips a cheap block; the agent's own block in hand also counts.
- Nav waits for the bridge to finish crossing, then resumes to the goal.

## No more "computes forever" on an unreachable goal (#50)

A goal on a non-standable cell — floating in the air, or the upper block of 2-tall grass
(a click reports the plant's own cell, which has no floor) — made tungsten's `;goto`
search re-root near it forever without ever moving. Two fixes:

- **Goal snap.** `;goto` and click-to-goto now snap a non-standable target to the
  reachable ground (the surface above a solid block, or down beneath the air/plant), so
  the common case resolves to a real, reachable target instead of spinning.
- **Search stall-cap + arrival stop.** The re-root machinery reset the search timeout on
  every re-plan, so a goal it can never reach (over the void, no blocks) re-rooted
  forever. The search now tracks *real* progress (an emitted partial or a block-path
  advance, never a bare re-root) and gives up after 20s without any; and a `;goto` stops
  its search the instant the bot reaches the goal instead of lingering.

Tested: `goal_air_test` (air / tall-grass goals snap and settle; a sky island across an
unreachable void gives up), `bridge_goto_test` (crosses a sky-island void by bridging),
plus terrain / break regression.

## Known / tracked

- 2-block vertical walls onto a ledge (terrain_test course C) still need a place-to-climb
  variant (pillar beside the wall) — not yet wired.
- The bridge fires on the give-up (a few seconds' stall) rather than the instant the gap
  is seen, and paves flat at the starting height.
