# 0.95.47 — nights

## Added

- **Night shelter.** With no bed at night on the surface, the bot digs a 1x1 hole two deep, drops
  in, caps it and waits until the sun has been up a while (time 1000), then goes back to work.
  Underground the night changes nothing and mining goes on. `;settings survival night_shelter 0` turns
  it off.
- **A bed from sheep by day.** Once it has an iron pickaxe, the bot collects wool for a bed when
  sheep are within 48 blocks, at most three minutes a day.
- **Grouped settings.** `;settings` lists groups, `;settings visuals` lists one group,
  `;settings visuals blocks_place 0` sets one value (0/1, on/off, or a number). Visuals: `all`,
  `movement_lines`, `route`, `mining`, `blocks_break`, `blocks_place`, `combat`.

## Fixed

- A MobDefense fight no longer follows its target out of the range in which it would have started
  one (10 blocks; archers 20 with a shield, 35 without, plus 6).
- An animal that cannot be reached is written off after 90 s (a rabbit on a ravine ledge took five
  minutes and ended in a fall).
- A bed placed a moment ago counts as a bed: the bot used to dig a night shelter two blocks from the
  bed it had just placed, because the block scanner had not seen it yet.
- A flight from mobs that is still being hit after 3 s is stood down for a fight when the bot has a
  weapon. Two zombies followed an unarmoured bot through a cave and killed it (20 -> 0 in 45 s)
  while it kept "fleeing"; on the same checkpoint both are now dead within 15 s.
- The bench counts deaths on the server too (scoreboard): a death on the death screen used to
  leave no LAVA/DEATH line and no pinned checkpoint.

## Validation

- Night on the rung-bucket checkpoint, no bed: 20 -> 1.7 health before, 20 all night after.
- full48 (38 min) and full51 (41 min, clean): no deaths; iron tools at 22 minutes in full51.
- Bed: at dusk with a bed in the pack the bot places it and sleeps; the night is skipped.
- Mob courses: melee, skeleton, weapon_swap and the three endermen courses pass.

## Known

- mob_skeleton and mob_trio still take arrows; mob_unarmed does not flee.
- Before the first bed, nights cost about 9 minutes in every 20 spent in the shelter.
