# 0.95.37 — route margins from lava and lethal drops; never dig your own floor over a drop

Three changes aimed at the nether deaths that remained after 0.95.34 put hazard checks in every
executor. Each came from a measured death shape; **none of them has yet been measured to raise the
nether survival rate**, and this note says so rather than implying it.

## What changed

- **FastPlanner prices a lava-adjacent cell at 12 steps** (other hazards keep 2x). The remaining lava
  entries were the body ending one block off a lava-free route (route-relative snapshot d1.0, d1.4,
  d1.0) -- executor drift, which baritone's discrete cell-to-cell executor does not have.
- **CombatPathfinder (the walker's main route source) searches with a margin first**: cells next to
  lava, or next to the lip of a drop of 20+ blocks, are closed; if that route does not reach the goal
  the bare search runs and is taken when IT does. The cliff half came from a death where the walker
  left a ledge at walking pace and fell 44 blocks into lava. (A first cut kept partial margin routes
  to save the second pass and froze nav_cliff at the lip; corrected before this release.)
- **DestroyBlockTask never digs its own floor over an unsafe landing** -- baritone MineProcess only
  mines at or above the feet in its own column (MineProcess.java:126). From a death recorded
  standing, velocity straight down, no movement driver at all.

Everything from 0.95.34 on is far better than the control, but between 0.95.34 and 0.95.37 the
difference is inside the spread of stints this short and this few (checklist 4b). The death shapes
are now all different from each other -- executor-driven drops, a walker jump-up next to a cliff with
no landing check (`canJump` skips the check when the waypoint is higher), a drop while the executor
drove on a non-full floor block. The next step is a deterministic course for these, not more stints.

Margin counters: lavaMargin kept/dropped ~13k/3.9k over the series, cliff closures ~357k; underfoot
refusals 0 (the underfoot rule did not fire in these stints).
