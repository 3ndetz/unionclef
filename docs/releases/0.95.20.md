# 0.95.20 — an unreachable food top-up no longer deadlocks the nether (G106 completion)

Follow-up to 0.95.19. Completes G106: the bot proceeds to the nether with an adequate food buffer
instead of hoarding to the full target when no more food is reachable.

## The deadlock

`BeatMinecraftTask` gates "Going to Nether" behind every gather being satisfied, and food is a
gather with target `foodUnits` (140). `CollectFoodPriorityCalculator` returned a weak-but-positive
0.1 priority whenever food was UNREACHABLE (`Double.isInfinite(distance)`) and the reserve was below
140 — which keeps CollectFood selected over the nether fall-through. On terrain where the animals are
gone (a resumed 22-minute world, a stripped area), the bot then "Collect 140 food / Wander for
Infinity" forever and never builds the portal. Measured on the `nether-reach` checkpoint: the run
stalled on food, 0 portal.

This is the other half of G106. That entry already lowered `foodUnits` 220 -> 140 and set
`minFoodUnits = 120` as the "solid buffer it tops up, not hoards" floor — but the code never
implemented "proceed at the floor": it blocked until the full 140 regardless of whether more food
could be reached.

## The fix

`CollectFoodPriorityCalculator` now takes `minFoodUnits`. On the unreachable-food branch it keeps
blocking the nether (0.1, and keeps exploring) only while the reserve is BELOW the survival floor;
once the reserve is adequate (>= `minFoodUnits`) but the top-up is unreachable, it stands down
(`NEGATIVE_INFINITY`) so progression fires. Reachable food (finite distance) still tops up to
`foodUnits` normally; `needsEmergencyFood` (+inf) and the low-reserve ramp still guard survival.

## Validation

Resumed the `nether-reach` checkpoint (survival server, natural terrain) with the fix:

- Before (0.95.19): stalled on "Collect 140 food / Wander for Infinity", no rung past `nether`.
- After: the bot stands down the unreachable top-up and PROGRESSES — flint, the `nether` rung
  (obsidian + flint & steel) at 133.6 s, wood, and on to **"Building nether portal with obsidian ->
  Making obsidian by flooding lava"** on natural terrain. hp stayed 20 throughout (it proceeded with
  an adequate buffer, not starving). `GAMER_SMOKE: PASS`, client fps 27.

This is the first time the natural-terrain portal path has been exercised end to end past the
resource gathers. It exposed the next frontier (below), which is a separate nav problem, not food.

## Known next frontier (not this fix)

On natural terrain the flood-gather then stalls "Approaching lava to flood": the lava source it
picked is deep underground (y=27) directly below the bot at the surface (y=61), and the nav does not
dig the ~34 blocks down to it. That is a nav/dig-to-a-deep-target problem, tracked as the next pass.
The portal BUILD itself is validated on the bench (isolated 8/8, full flood 6/6, v0.95.19).
