# 0.95.52 — arrows, and the lava lake

## Fixed

- **The arrow dodge looks where it steps.** Each tick an arrow is close, the dodge simulates
  eighteen key combinations (eight strafes and standing still, with and without a jump) with
  tungsten's physics agent against the arrow's own flight. It refuses any that touch what baritone
  refuses to walk into (fluids, magma, cactus, berry bush, fire, cobweb) or drop more than three
  blocks, and keeps the one that clears the arrow best. `TungstenConfig.dodgeSearch = false`
  turns it off for comparison.
- **Next to lava or an edge the bot dodged by standing still.** Danger zones handed the legs to a
  route-search task that never moved in time; they now use the same search.
- A settings file with `dodgeProjectiles: false` says so in chat on every load (issue #34).
- The bucket portal looks for its lava lake in the world (radius 64), not only among the 40 nearest
  lava blocks the scanner keeps. full57 spent 20 minutes next to single lava sources.
- A bucket-cast mould cell with nothing to place against gets a throwaway support (cobblestone or
  dirt, the way baritone's pillar and bridge moves use them) before the mould.

## Validation

- arrow_dodge (flat, 10 arrows at the bot's position): 0 damage, 3/3. Dodge off: 24 hp, 3/3.
- arrow_dodge_ledge (two-wide ledge, lava one side, a six-block drop the other):
  route task 28-36 hp in every run; physics search without jumps 20-24 hp; with jumps 0 hp in 4 of 6,
  4 hp in two. No lava and no falls in any run.
- mob_skeleton: min_hp 20, 16, 16 (before: 16, 20).

## Known

- The bucket portal still cannot cast its upper frame (portal_lava_lake reaches about 10
  obsidian, the top side cell stalls). Without a diamond pickaxe a playthrough stops there.
