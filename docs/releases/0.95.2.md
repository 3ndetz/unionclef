# 0.95.2 — out of the pit, out of the shaft

A patch on 0.95.1, cut after the first sixty-minute playthrough on it. That run is the first
to go past iron: shield, bucket, water bucket, diamonds from a shaft to y=7, a diamond pickaxe,
diamond boots and a diamond chestplate — 747 items, no deaths. It also spent twenty-nine of its
sixty minutes standing in two holes, and both stands were the navigator's hand-off refusing or
mis-routing the tower that would have taken it out. Both are fixed here.

---

## The two fixes

- **A goal that is below but far is reached by leaving the hole (G97).** 0.94's rule "a tower
  toward a goal that is below you is the plan going the wrong way" (G60) had its coal straight
  under the feet. The sixty-minute run sat nine minutes in a pit with iron ore three blocks
  lower and twenty-five blocks away: the plan was complete and climbed 4.5 out of the pit
  first, and the refusal fired 115 times until every ore in reach was marked unreachable. The
  refusal keeps its shape and gains a horizontal radius (`noTowerWhenGoalIsBelowRadius`, 6
  blocks; `navTowerAllowedFar` counts the towers it now allows).
- **A short partial plan that carries a tower is a leg, not a dead end (G98).** The run ended
  with twenty minutes at the bottom of its own diamond shaft, the next goal nine blocks straight
  up. Every plan was partial — two ledges, then flagged tower cells — and under the five blocks
  the walk-the-partial rule wants, so the goal went to the physics engine, which has no place
  move: "no progress … giving the route up", sixty-four times. A partial that carries a flagged
  build or dig is now walked to it, and the flagged hand-off (PillarTask for a tower, the
  navigator's own dig for a break run) does the rest (`navPartialBuild`).

**HOW TO TEST:** `python deploy/runner/shaft_exit_test.py` (a 2x1 shaft ten deep cut into a
stone mound, the goal on top two blocks aside): out in 6.5 s on this build, 9.9 s at
`--depth 22`, 10.1 s at `--depth 22 --budget-ms 20`. Honest note: on the open mound the plan
completes even with the planner starved to 20 ms, so the bench proves the tower hand-off from a
shaft, not the partial branch itself — that one is covered by the run's log signature
(`PLAN … complete=false … flagged=3` → `navPartialBuild`) and waits for a scene that reproduces
the partial (the planner's pillar generation, recorded under G98). `python deploy/runner/
pit_escape_test.py` still passes (the up-and-to-the-side goal). The nav regression suite on this
build: **14 of 14, no invalid runs**.

## Recorded, not started

- The planner expanded 28 pillar moves against 100k climb nodes in those shaft searches; a
  tower move priced or ordered so that a plan out of a ten-deep shaft never completes (noted
  under G98).
- The night job (G95) still picks blocks far below the surface; in this run the bot mined
  through it without harm, with diamond armour on.
- After iron tools the raw-iron gatherer's "Retrying old heuristic" and the chain's "No tasks.
  Time to add new!" alternated for thirteen minutes (a consequence of G97's refusals; watch
  whether it recurs without them).
