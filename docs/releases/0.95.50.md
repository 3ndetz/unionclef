# 0.95.50 — look further, dig faster

## Fixed

- **Food search walked in circles.** With nothing in sight on the surface the bot used a spiral
  32-56 blocks round where it started, i.e. the same ground again. It now walks outward in 48-block
  legs and turns only when a leg stops gaining (ExploreOutwardTask; baritone's ExploreProcess goes
  outward the same way). On the full52 checkpoint: pigs found in under three minutes, none in five
  before; fed and at full health, no deaths in ten minutes.
- **Every dig run waited 0.6 s for sand to fall**, stone included. It waits only when a falling
  block is there now. nav_tunnel (ten blocks of stone): 31 s -> 23 s.
- **"Reset the bed" walked to any bed it knew of** after 30 s awake at night (full53: ~80 blocks,
  twice, two minutes). Only a bed within 8 blocks now, and not after tonight's sleep budget.

## Added

- nav_tunnel and mob_pigs courses; py4j digStats (dig runs, mining time, pause after each dig).

## Validation

- nav 18/18 (nav_tunnel included); mob 8/10 on 0.95.49 with trio and unarmed failing as before.
- full53 from zero: iron tools at ~10 minutes (22 in full51 and full52), no deaths in 37 minutes;
  then stuck on food and a bed walk, which is what this release fixes; the pig hunt on its own
  checkpoint now takes about a minute.

## Known

- The smoker task walks to an old smoker ~75 blocks away when its cache says the smoker holds
  anything (SmeltInSmokerTask.getCostToMakeNew).
