# 0.88.0 — instruments that can tell "nothing happened" from "it never ran"

Mostly tooling. The one behaviour change of 0.87.0 (critical hits) is unchanged and now has a
second course backing it.

## The damage instruments

`dealt` used to print `0.0` through a fight with seventy swings in it, because the ledger was
wired to a caller that pvp courses never run. It now ticks from the client tick and reports what
a hit actually looked like, from both ends of the exchange:

```
dealt=<ours>/<all>/<ledger ticks>
hitSize=<chip>/<partial>/<flat>/<crit>      what our hits landed for
takeSize=<chip>/<partial>/<flat>/<crit>     what WE took, read from our own health
```

Buckets are the vanilla quantities: a full iron_sword blow is 6.0 and a crit 9.0. The third
number in `dealt` is the tick count, and it exists so a zero can never again be read as "found
nothing" — if it is 0, the ledger did not run.

**HOW TO TEST.** Hit anything and watch `dealt` move (`@stats`, or `placeStats` over py4j). Then
check the shape: against an unarmoured target most hits should land in the `flat` bucket, and
falling swings in `crit`. `chipScenes` names any hit under 2 hp with its context.

Damage against players and non-skeleton mobs was invisible before this, so numbers in artifacts
from before 2026-08-20 are not comparable with later ones.

## Critical hits, confirmed on a second course

0.87.0 shipped the sprint reset on `allround` evidence (+1.0 kills a run, t=3.16). The obvious
worry was `edge_duel` — a small platform over void, where dropping sprint costs the knockback
that pushes people off. Six interleaved runs there: margin **+1.33 with it on** against −1.67
with it off, gate 2/3 against 1/3. The crit is worth more than the knockback it costs.

**HOW TO TEST.** `python deploy/runner/run_suite.py pvp --only edge_duel --no-early-stop --repeat 6
--pin-alt critReleasesSprint=false`, then compare the `won the exchange` line between arms.

## Flags, all default OFF

Each measured, each neutral or worse, kept because the reasoning is sound and the numbers are
recorded at the flag:

- `syncSlotToServer` — send the hotbar packet at the switch instead of on the next tick.
- `reassertSlotOnRespawn` — re-send the held slot after a respawn.
- `holdSwingTicksAfterSwitch` — do not swing for N ticks after a weapon change. Removes the
  1.0-damage hits completely (20.3 → 1.0) and gains no damage; they come back as partials.
- `disengageOnLosingExchange` — trigger the wounded retreat on losing the exchange rather than on
  low health. Fires correctly, loses more.
- `woundedHoldsInsteadOfKiting`, `equipMeleeOnApproach`, `punkRestartKeepsFight`.

## Bench

- `--swap-alt` swaps the two containers' roles on alternate runs, which tells a code asymmetry
  from a stand asymmetry on mirror courses.
- `--scn-alt` interleaves the SCENARIO's own strategy, so a driver can be A/B'd like a setting.
- `--pin-base` gives the arm-A value for a non-boolean `--pin-alt`.
- `allround`'s opponent now gets the same kit as the bot. It had been given an iron_sword welded
  into `weapon.mainhand` while the bot paid for every weapon switch, which was worth −5.67 margin
  on its own. The course passes now.

## Known bugs

- `edge_duel` is flaky on its own gate (5 PASS / 3 FAIL since 2026-08-12).
- The playthrough still tops out around stone tools.
