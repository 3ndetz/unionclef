# 0.95.34 — hazard safety ported from baritone: one predicate, checked by every planner AND every executor

Supersedes the untagged 0.95.30-0.95.33. Everything they changed is in this jar; the per-driver
lava gates of 0.95.31/0.95.32 are gone, replaced by the port below.

## Why

Since 0.95.29 the bot builds a portal and enters the nether on natural terrain. Once there it kept
dying in lava. The lava-entry instrument (py4j `lavaEntryStats()`, extended twice this release)
settled what was actually happening, and it was not what was first written down:

- The first reading ("an Enderman knocks the body off a ledge") was wrong. Its own signature had
  zero horizontal speed, which argues against knockback, and the combat pipeline was not driving
  at all (`vgCalls=0`, `reposition=0`).
- With the fall vector and the driver recorded, the deaths were the MOVEMENT EXECUTORS walking or
  stepping the body into lava: the waypoint walker (`walker1`, level ground, `onGround=1`), then
  the physics replay executor (`exec1`), then the MovementQueue (`nav1`, walker and executor off).

Each driver was patched in turn, and each time the next driver was the way in. That is the
mistake baritone does not make, and it was on the shelf the whole time.

## What baritone does, and what this ports

- **One predicate.** `MovementHelper.avoidWalkingInto` (lava, magma, fire, cactus, cobweb, berry
  bush, bubble column...). Here: `RouteHazards.hazard`, the same list minus water (tungsten swims).
  `FastPlanner.hazardAt` now delegates to it, so planner and executors cannot disagree.
- **Every movement's cost refuses it**, including a column that ends in lava (Descend/Fall with
  `allowFallIntoLava=false`, Parkour's gap floor). Here: `RouteHazards.lethalColumn`.
- **Diagonals do not shave a hazardous corner** (`MovementDiagonal` :158/162, :188/189). tungsten's
  diagonal checked only collision, and lava has none. `FastPlanner` diagonals now apply the rule.
- **The executor re-checks the next movements every tick and cancels on an impossible one**
  (`PathExecutor` :196-210). Here: `RouteHazards.segmentLethal`, the stretch from the body to the
  next waypoint/node/movement and on, called every tick by the walker (both modes), the physics
  replay executor and the MovementQueue. Lethal on the ground means release the keys and re-plan
  from where the body stands.

| 0.95.32 (per-driver gates) | 2 of 2 completed stints, under `exec1` and `nav1`/`pf1` |
