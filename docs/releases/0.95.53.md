# 0.95.53 — a portal without a diamond pickaxe

## Fixed

- **The bucket portal kept its site.** A stall on one frame cell used to drop the whole site; the
  new site was one block over, so the obsidian already cast sat inside the new opening, where an
  iron pickaxe cannot remove it. A site with obsidian in its frame is now kept.
- **The cast never mines obsidian.** It read "is this obsidian" through the block scanner, which
  answers no for any cell marked unreachable, and then tried to break its own obsidian. It reads
  the world now, and obsidian is never a clearing target.
- **A mould block beside a side face gets a one-block pillar to stand on**, as baritone's
  GoalAdjacent allows, instead of a walk to the top of a two-high mould column it cannot climb.
  After one failed walk to a stand, a pillar stand is preferred for that cell.
- **Swimming out of ankle-deep water.** At a wall too high to jump, the navigator swam if the body
  touched any water, and bobbed for minutes in the run-off of a cast. It swims only in a column of
  water now and pillars otherwise (baritone's MovementPillar).
- The cast returned a fresh wander on every stall check; it ran for one tick and tore down the
  mould placement every six seconds. One wander now runs to the end.
- A block placement whose aim never lands is handed back after three seconds; one attempt at one
  block gets 40 s whatever the body is doing.
- Bot messages are English ("Task finished in 12.3 s."); the project language is English.

## Validation

- portal_lava_lake (lava lake, buckets, no diamond pickaxe): portal lit in 1 of 8 runs on 0.95.52;
  5 of 9 over the last three series on this build, in 125-155 s. The bot walks into the nether.
- nav 18/18, craft 24/24.

## Known

- The frame's top corner can still stall: the bot ends up in cast water in a one-block pit and the
  wander does not get it out (about one run in three).

## How to test

```
python3 deploy/runner/run_suite.py craft --only portal_lava_lake --repeat 3 --record
```
