# Dressed for the nether

Three changes for the night and the nether, each measured on the bench.

**The bot no longer floats in a lake all night.** With every dig-in site within 16 blocks under
water, the night shelter used to wait in the lake till morning -- 6.5 minutes afloat in one run,
"impaled by Drowned" in another. It now heads for dry land (a heightmap scan out to 48 blocks)
and digs in there. Lake replays: before, 0/4 left the water; after, the valid runs reached the
shore and dug in.

**No hoglin hunting in stone gear.** A hoglin has 40 health and throws the player on every hit;
from the nether arrival, hunts with a stone sword ended "slain by Hoglin" or "fell from a high
place". Hoglins are now food only with an iron-or-better weapon and an iron-or-better chestplate
worn; otherwise the bot goes back to the Overworld for food. Same checkpoint: deaths 2/2 before,
1/2 after (that one from a piglin fight).

**A gold helmet before the nether.** Piglins attack a player wearing no gold. Before the trip the
bot now mines, smelts and crafts a gold helmet, with a six-minute budget so a world without gold
nearby does not stop the run. Two 15-minute runs: a helmet in both, no deaths; one reached the
nether and was alive at full health for its remaining seven minutes there.

**HOW TO TEST:**

```powershell
python deploy/runner/gamer_smoke.py 15 --from full59-before-portal --raw-resume --record
python deploy/runner/gamer_smoke.py 6 --from nether-fresh --raw-resume --record
```

Also: `InputControls.waterKeyHolders` names whoever holds jump or sneak in water, and
`gamer_smoke.py --grcon "COMMAND"` runs an rcon command after a checkpoint restore.

**Known:** in the nether the bot later holds a small area on "Going to biome"; tracked. No speed
or FPS gain is claimed, and the whole playthrough is still open.
