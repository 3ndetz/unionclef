# 0.95.8 — food stops burying progression after iron

A patch on 0.95.7, from the day-locked probes after iron: the bot progresses (deep mining,
nether prep) but the food goal keeps it in the overworld far too long before it will leave
for the nether.

---

## The fix (G106)

Two food-priority problems, both measured on the day-locked runs:

1. **A hay bale within 75 blocks multiplied the WHOLE food goal by 50**, driving its priority
   to ~1293 near a village — food then buried diamond and nether-prep tasks (which sit at
   15–40) for the entire stage. A hay bale is efficient food (9 wheat → 9 bread), worth a
   boost, but roughly seven animal kills, not thirty. The multiplier is now 7.

2. **The nether departure was gated behind 220 food units (~27 cooked meats).** The main loop
   only falls through to "Going to Nether" when every gather task is satisfied, and food at
   220 is a whole-journey stockpile — gathering it all up front kept a 25-minute probe in
   overworld prep without ever leaving. The bot re-collects food whenever it drops below the
   target, and the emergency-food path ramps to infinity when it is actually low, so a smaller
   buffer is topped up, not risked. The target is now 140 (~17 meats) — still a nether trip
   with margin, reached sooner.

## HOW TO TEST

`python deploy/runner/gamer_smoke.py 12 --from cp0918-1030-t494 --daylock --record` — the bot
should complete nether prep and reach "Going to Nether" (or get much closer) within the
window, and food/hp should stay healthy (no starvation). Before the change, a 25-minute probe
from the same checkpoint stayed in overworld prep.

## Known, not fixed

- The night track (death = base loss) stays deferred by the operator.
- Nether-stage progression (fortress, blaze, pearls) is the next frontier once the bot leaves
  reliably.
