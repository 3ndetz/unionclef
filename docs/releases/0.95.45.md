# 0.95.45 — no more thrown pearls, food in the nether for real

Found on 45-minute nether runs from rung-ender with the enderman pillar in.

## Fixed

- **A block placement could throw what was in the hand.** `BlockPlaceHelper.tryPlace` fell back to
  using the held item, as baritone's does for buckets; with an ender pearl in the main hand it threw
  the pearl and the bot teleported 27 blocks off its pillar. The fallback is for buckets only.
- **Armour that was already worn was equipped every tick.** `hasItem` counts the armour slots, so
  the main task returned a finished EquipArmorTask forever and never reached its nether logic
  (five minutes standing at 0.6 health by the lava sea).
- **Food in the nether, in the task that actually runs.** The main task (beatgame) now fetches food
  in the nether when the stock is under 16 units: hoglins, or back to the Overworld.
- **Enderman pillar:** a plant in the feet cell is cleared first (a warped fungus caught the
  placement ray for ten minutes); a pillar that does not rise is abandoned; an enderman counts as
  reachable only while its health falls (ten minutes of swings into the pillar's own edge); swings
  aim at its head; sites need ground two blocks out (a fungus crown with a thirty-block drop).

## Validation

- n58, 45 min from rung-ender: no deaths, 9/14 pearls, a food trip to the Overworld on its own.
- n57, 45 min: no deaths (5 pearls, before the reachability fix).
- nav 17/17; mob_endermen_shelter 2/2, mob_endermen 2/2.

## Known

- Back in the Overworld the main task wants its full food reserve before returning to the nether.
- Pearl rate is still below the open fight's.
