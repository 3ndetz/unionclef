# 0.95.27 — the builder returns to the frame when it has strayed VERTICALLY, not only horizontally

Follow-up to 0.95.23 (which keeps the builder at the build) and 0.95.25. Note: 0.95.26 was a lava
clamp that froze the bot beside a lake and was reverted before release; the number stays retired.

## The stall

A recorded `@gamer` run from the deep `nether-reach` checkpoint flooded a lava lake, mined all 14
obsidian it needed (hp 20 throughout) and started the frame -- then spent its last four minutes
stationary against a wall at y=13, with `PlaceBlockTask: Place obsidian at (732,69,832) -> Wandering /
Failed exploring`. The frame origin had been chosen on the SURFACE (y=69) while the bot was up there;
it then mined its obsidian deep and ended at (731.7,13,833.7): 1.4 blocks from the origin horizontally,
**56 blocks below it**. The portal that had every block in hand was never placed, and the run ended
in the overworld. (Frames verified before this was written.)

## The root

0.95.23's return-to-build guard measured X/Z only (`ddx² + ddz² > 100`), so a body standing directly
UNDER the frame's column was "at the build" to it and it never fired. `PlaceBlockTask` then fell to its
`TimeoutWanderTask` recovery, which cannot climb 56 blocks.

## The fix

`ConstructNetherPortalObsidianTask` now also measures the vertical separation: more than six blocks
above or below the origin (a frame is five tall, its stands within a couple of blocks) counts as
strayed, and the builder walks back to the origin first, exactly as for a horizontal drift. One
condition added to the existing guard; a no-op whenever the body is within the frame's own column of
height, so it cannot regress the flat bench or the surface build.

## Second fix in this release: the IN-RANGE flood rim gets a shimmy-proof guard

The recorded validation run for the fix above froze at the flood instead -- at (789.5,20,818.6), the
same spot as the run that got 0.95.26 reverted, but on a jar with no clamp at all. Probed live: the
flood had committed to rim (789,21,816), 2.7 blocks from the body (lava 2.4 blocks away); that is IN
RANGE, so 0.95.24's approach guard rests, and `InteractWithBlockTask` sat in "Getting within reach"
for 16k ticks because the rim's top face cannot be reached from the pocket under the lake -- while the
`UnstuckChain` owned the bot every tick (own 11938 -> 12147 in 15 s) and its shimmy reset the only
other guard, the displacement checker. 0.95.24 closed the far rim and left the near, unplaceable rim
open; which rim the flood picks is why the same lake floods on one run and freezes on the next.

`PlaceObsidianFloodTask` now guards the in-range case by the goal itself: if the water has not landed
after 200 flood ticks in range, the rim is blacklisted and the flood re-selects (and with every
reachable-looking rim exhausted, explores for a lake it can flood from solid ground). Same design as
the approach guard -- a bound on the goal never being reached, not a timer on the body -- so the
shimmy cannot fake it. It also withdraws the earlier claim that the 0.95.26 clamp caused this freeze.

## Validation

- Deterministic repro: the recorded run's tail (bot at y=13 under an origin at y=69).
- Recorded nether-reach re-run on this jar: **no freeze at the flood** (the near-rim deadlock did not
  recur; the flood picked the deep lake, `UnstuckChain` ownership 0, obsidian mined safely, deaths 0,
  fps 29, GAMER_SMOKE PASS) -- but it ran out of its 32-min window at 12/17 obsidian (deep basin,
  slow collection), so the frame/entry were not exercised in that run. Three recorded natural runs
  now missed the entry for three different reasons (freeze / vertical gap / window); the two logged
  entries were ~28 min. The vertical-return and frame path are validated on a deterministic entry
  demo below (obsidian given by command, build + light + enter).
- Entry demo (obsidian + flint&steel given via rcon after restore, recorded, frames verified before
  sending): **the vertical return FIRED on film** ("Returning to the portal build" at t=254 s, the
  builder walking back to the frame at (735,68,829)), the frame went up at y=69 with the front
  scaffold for the top row, the portal LIT, the bot ENTERED THE NETHER (t=269 s) and began the nether
  stage ("Hunting endermen for pearls", diamond sword in hand, t=329 s). GAMER_SMOKE PASS, fps 28.
  It then died to lava in the nether (~t=400 s) -- the documented nether-stage frontier, not this fix.
  The entry hook also refreshed the `nether-fresh` checkpoint from this run.
- Regression, flat obsidian flood (OBS bench): **PASS** -- `NETHER_PORTAL LIT at (2358,-56,358)` in
  203 s, real portal block. Both guards are no-ops there (the flat flood's rim is placeable in range,
  the frame is within its own column of height). Nav baselines `nav_flat` / `nav_staircase` /
  `nav_descend`: **3/3 PASS**, 0 gate failures, 0 invalid runs.
