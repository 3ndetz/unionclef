# 0.95.27 — the builder returns to the frame when it has strayed VERTICALLY, not only horizontally

Follow-up to 0.95.23 (which keeps the builder at the build) and 0.95.25. Note: 0.95.26 was a lava
clamp that froze the bot beside a lake and was reverted before release; the number stays retired.

## The stall

A recorded `@gamer` run from the deep `nether-reach` checkpoint flooded a lava lake, mined all 14
obsidian it needed (hp 20 throughout) and started the frame -- then spent its last four minutes
stationary against a wall at y=13, with `PlaceBlockTask: Place obsidian at (732,69,832) -> Wandering /
Failed exploring`. The frame origin had been chosen on the SURFACE (y=69) while the bot was up there;
it then mined its obsidian deep and ended at (731.7,13,833.7): 1.4 blocks from the origin horizontally,
**56 blocks below it**. The portal that had every block in hand was never placed, and the run ended
in the overworld. (Frames verified before this was written.)

## The root

0.95.23's return-to-build guard measured X/Z only (`ddx² + ddz² > 100`), so a body standing directly
UNDER the frame's column was "at the build" to it and it never fired. `PlaceBlockTask` then fell to its
`TimeoutWanderTask` recovery, which cannot climb 56 blocks.

## The fix

`ConstructNetherPortalObsidianTask` now also measures the vertical separation: more than six blocks
above or below the origin (a frame is five tall, its stands within a couple of blocks) counts as
strayed, and the builder walks back to the origin first, exactly as for a horizontal drift. One
condition added to the existing guard; a no-op whenever the body is within the frame's own column of
height, so it cannot regress the flat bench or the surface build.
