# 0.95.42 — the nether from the checkpoint: no more lava deaths, and the config that hid every new default

Everything here came from 20-45 minute nether runs resumed from the `rung-ender` checkpoint, with each
death reproduced before it was fixed.

## Fixed

- **tungsten.json froze every default.** It was written as a full dump, so one `;settings` kept every
  old value forever and new defaults in the code never reached that machine; the playthrough bench
  never reset it either. Now only values that differ from the shipped defaults are written (old full
  files are discarded once), and `gamer_smoke` starts on the shipped defaults like `run_suite`.
- **A 20-minute stall at 4 hp** ("Collect food -> Wander") was an idle tungsten lock: 20871 of 22981
  locked ticks had nothing driving. The release for exactly that existed and was off; now on.
- **Food was thrown away as garbage** when the inventory filled (26 sweet berries in a minute in the
  nether), and of two food stacks the most valuable went first. Food now goes last, least valuable first.
- **The replay executor brakes on its body's actual motion** into a lethal column, not only on the
  planned nodes (a drift off a lip, 23 blocks into lava).
- **A speed hop is not safe over a shallow lava pool** (lava has no collision, so the landing check
  read "fall 2"); every column of the hop now answers to the executors' lethal rule.
- **No new enderman when two of its hits kill**; heal or eat first.
- **The edge guard is armed in a melee with a mob** (knocked off a rim 22 blocks up by an enderman).
- **Eat to keep regeneration running** when hurt and under 18 food.

## Bench

- `gamer_smoke` prints every lava entry and death with its snapshot (driver, route geometry, task,
  last break) as it happens, stops the bot after its verdict (a "nether lava death" turned out to
  happen between two runs), logs hunger and food per sample.
- New courses: `mob_endermen`, `mob_endermen_hurt` (guards: both pass 4/4, they do not discriminate).

## Validation

- nav 16/17 + nav_gaps 2/2 on both flag arms; chase_flat, chase_terrain, bridge_assault 3/3; mob_melee,
  mob_weapon_swap, mob_unarmed PASS; mob_trio and mob_skeleton fail on the control too.
- Nether from rung-ender, 20-minute runs: lava deaths gone (4 of 4 before); best run 20 min with no
  death and 12 of 14 pearls. Enderman fights are the killer now (7 of the last 10 deaths).

## Known

- Enderman fights in the nether's terrain; standing lava death seen once; water escape without tools.

python3 deploy/runner/run_suite.py nav
