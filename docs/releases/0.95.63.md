# Water, air and buckets

Work from the UNIONCLEF-HARD session (developed and bench-tested 2026-10-02..10-04, left
uncommitted when that session paused), reviewed, rebuilt over 0.95.62 and re-tested before
release.

**Pillaring in water uses swimming input.** A pillar started in water used to hold sneak with
jump, and sneak sinks the body: the bot sat at a bank holding both. It now swims up while in
water and goes back to the land sneak/jump placement once out. `pillarUsesSwimInputInWater` is
now on by default (`false` keeps the old input). Earlier evidence: 42 water/neighbour and 36 dry
interaction trials passed at 29+ FPS; on the retained food-bank checkpoint new food appeared in
5/5 FPS-valid ON observations against 1/5 OFF.

**The air escape starts when the way out needs it.** The rescue used to wait until half the air
was gone. It now prices the actual exit -- digging the cap overhead, with vanilla's underwater
mining penalty, or swimming to the nearest breathable cell -- and starts early enough to finish
it. A live route to air is followed to its end instead of being re-priced every tick (baritone
PathingBehavior.java:196-224), which stops the dig/swim flip-flop measured in dry-entry tunnels.

**Extra water buckets are emptied, one is kept.** The task used to empty every water bucket at
its own feet. It now keeps one filled bucket and empties only the extras, against a solid face
within reach.

**The block scanner no longer reads the live world from its worker thread.** Chunk contents are
copied on the client tick (`ChunkBlockSnapshot`) and the worker searches the copies.

Re-tested on this build (0.95.62 + this work):

- `nav_water`, `nav_water_pillar`, `nav_grid_water_roof`, `nav_flat`, `nav_staircase`,
  `nav_descend`, `nav_break`, `nav_bridge`, `nav_gaps`: 9/9 PASS, 29-30 FPS.
- `portal_lava_lake` PASS (27.6 FPS), `craft_at_distant_table` PASS.
- Same checkpoint 7 minutes before a drowning in the air escape, interleaved: 0.95.62 had two
  deaths in one of two runs (a creeper and a zombie, lowest health 1.8); this build none in two
  (lowest health 11.2 and 20). The drowning itself did not recur in either arm, so this is not
  claimed as a drowning fix.
- Night in a lake (`repro-lake-night`): no deaths, health 20.
- 15-minute native run from `full59-before-portal`: PASS, 0 deaths, lowest health 17.3, iron and
  crafting rungs. Its median FPS was 20; the interleaved checkpoint pair above read the same FPS on
  both builds (26/22 vs 26/22), so the host was loaded that day rather than the scanner slow.
