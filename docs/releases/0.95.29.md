# 0.95.29 — a portal pad the body cannot walk back to is re-sited, not walked to for ever

Ships the two fixes written for 0.95.28 as well (that version was built and benched but never
tagged): **lava counts as the void** in `VoidDetector.fallHeight`, and **no "escape to the surface"
under a roof**. Their notes are in `docs/releases/0.95.28.md`.

## The defect this release is named for

0.95.24-0.95.27 cleared the portal build one stall at a time, and each fix moved the ceiling to the
next thing. This one was introduced by 0.95.27 itself.

The frame site is reserved FIRST, while the bot still stands on undisturbed ground — that is
deliberate and the reasons are at the call site (siting after the gather put the frame in the mess
the flood had just made). 0.95.27 then added a guard: if the body has strayed from the reserved
origin during the build, walk it back. It assumed the walk is always possible.

On natural terrain it is not. The site is chosen wherever the portal task began, usually the
surface; the obsidian is then made by flooding a lava lake, which is tens of blocks DOWN a cave. The
guard fires by construction on every tick thereafter, for a walk the drive cannot make. Measured on
a 35-minute run from `nether-reach`:

```
origin (735,68,829)     body (799,4,846)     64 down, 64 across, in the water of its own flood
"Returning to the portal build" -> GetToBlockTask   for the LAST NINE MINUTES of the run
position frozen to the decimetre, 17 obsidian in the pack, no portal
```

The run before it lost its last four minutes to the horizontal twin of the same thing.

## The fix

The reservation is now provisional. While the return guard drives the body back, the squared
distance to the origin is tracked; when it stops getting closer for 200 ticks (~10 s — long enough
for a detour round a wall, far short of nine minutes), the pad is unreachable FROM HERE. The origin
is dropped and the site search runs again on the next tick from where the body actually is, which is
beside the pool it just made.

Siting there is safe now in a way it was not when "site first" was introduced: both site checks
(`isDecentBuildSite`, `isCleanFlatBuildSite`) reject obsidian, lava and water in the footprint, so
the search cannot land in the flood's mess — it lands on clean ground next to it. The
interior-clearing target is cleared with the origin, or it would send the bot back to the abandoned
site to break a block the moment the new frame closed.

A human who carried obsidian out of a cave and found the surface pad unreachable would build by the
cave mouth. So does the bot now.
