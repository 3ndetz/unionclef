# 0.95.29 — a portal pad the body cannot walk back to is re-sited, not walked to for ever

Ships the two fixes written for 0.95.28 as well (that version was built and benched but never
tagged): **lava counts as the void** in `VoidDetector.fallHeight`, and **no "escape to the surface"
under a roof**. Their notes are in `docs/releases/0.95.28.md`.

## The defect this release is named for

0.95.24-0.95.27 cleared the portal build one stall at a time, and each fix moved the ceiling to the
next thing. This one was introduced by 0.95.27 itself.

The frame site is reserved FIRST, while the bot still stands on undisturbed ground — that is
deliberate and the reasons are at the call site (siting after the gather put the frame in the mess
the flood had just made). 0.95.27 then added a guard: if the body has strayed from the reserved
origin during the build, walk it back. It assumed the walk is always possible.

On natural terrain it is not. The site is chosen wherever the portal task began, usually the
surface; the obsidian is then made by flooding a lava lake, which is tens of blocks DOWN a cave. The
guard fires by construction on every tick thereafter, for a walk the drive cannot make. Measured on
a 35-minute run from `nether-reach`:

```
origin (735,68,829)     body (799,4,846)     64 down, 64 across, in the water of its own flood
"Returning to the portal build" -> GetToBlockTask   for the LAST NINE MINUTES of the run
position frozen to the decimetre, 17 obsidian in the pack, no portal
```

The run before it lost its last four minutes to the horizontal twin of the same thing.

## The fix

The reservation is now provisional. While the return guard drives the body back, the squared
distance to the origin is tracked; when it stops getting closer for 200 ticks (~10 s — long enough
for a detour round a wall, far short of nine minutes), the pad is unreachable FROM HERE. The origin
is dropped and the site search runs again on the next tick from where the body actually is, which is
beside the pool it just made.

Siting there is safe now in a way it was not when "site first" was introduced: both site checks
(`isDecentBuildSite`, `isCleanFlatBuildSite`) reject obsidian, lava and water in the footprint, so
the search cannot land in the flood's mess — it lands on clean ground next to it. The
interior-clearing target is cleared with the origin, or it would send the bot back to the abandoned
site to break a block the moment the new frame closed.

A human who carried obsidian out of a cave and found the surface pad unreachable would build by the
cave mouth. So does the bot now.

## Two more layers this release also closes

Each was found by running the repro again after the previous fix, which is the only reason they were
found at all.

**The stray was measured against the origin POINT, and the frame is not symmetric about it.** The
frame spans `origin.y-1` (bottom row on the floor pad) to `origin.y+3` (top row). `|dy| > 6`
therefore called a body FOUR BLOCKS BELOW THE FRAME'S FLOOR "at the build". Measured with the
re-siting live: frame at (749,14,823), TEN of fourteen obsidian placed into a stone wall — read
straight out of the world over py4j — body at y=9, sealed in the cave below, unable to reach its
scaffold cell at (750,16,822). Nineteen minutes of UnstuckChain shimmy; the run ended 10/14. The
band is now the frame plus two blocks of slack each way.

**And the re-siting trigger was a stopwatch, which thrashed.** 200 ticks without net approach is not
what "unreachable" means on a cave route: the return leg is 35-57 blocks through rock, the drive
mines its way (`Mining done — passage open` throughout) and net distance does not fall while it
does. Three re-sites in five minutes at 35, 57 and 40 blocks — all mid-journey on walks that were
going fine — each throwing away the obsidian already placed and sending the bot back to gather.
`GetToBlockTask` already publishes the honest verdict (`onWander` →
`BlockScanner.requestBlockUnreachable`), so the re-site triggers on that; the tick count survives
only as a two-minute backstop, and the log line names which of the two fired.

## Validation

Three 35-minute runs from the same deterministic checkpoint (`nether-reach`: underground, y≈37,
before the portal), each one measuring the fix made after the previous.

- **The portal is BUILT and the NETHER is ENTERED on natural terrain, in one run, from underground.**
  `ladder: nether@134.1s, ender@1710.1s` — it went on to take an ender pearl in the nether. This is
  the post-iron ceiling this line of work was opened for.
- The freeze is gone: longest repeated position 110 s, against nine minutes (run 1) and nineteen
  minutes (run 2) on the two builds before it.
- Re-siting instrument fired correctly and only when earned: `Portal pad 727, 73, 826 is unreachable
  from 750, 13, 827` — a pad 60 blocks above the body — and the thrashing 35/57/40-block re-sites of
  the stopwatch version did not recur.
- 0 deaths during the portal phase on all three runs.
- Flat obsidian flood (OBS bench): PASS, portal lit in **240.9 s** on a fresh client (268.4 s on the
  previous jar). One run on a client that had just carried a 35-minute survival session read >500 s
  and did not finish in the window — re-run fresh it passed, which is this stand's documented client
  wear, not a regression: the guard this release changes cannot fire on the flat bench at all (the
  body never strays from the pad there).
- Nav baselines `nav_flat`, `nav_staircase`, `nav_descend`: 3/3 PASS, twice.

**What is NOT fixed, stated plainly.** The bot still dies in the nether: `tester1 tried to swim in
lava`, and the lava-entry instrument reads the same signature as before this release's lava-as-void
change — `hurtTime=6 onGround=0 speedCm=0 combatFwd=0 stage=NARROW_BATTLE`, i.e. knocked off a ledge
by an Enderman, not walking in. The diagnosis is now precise and is the next pass:
`SafetySystem.evaluateStage` returns `NARROW_BATTLE` before it ever tests the predicted
post-knockback fall, so `DANGER_BATTLE` — the one stage that repositions away from where a hit would
throw the body — cannot run on the terrain where being thrown is lethal.
