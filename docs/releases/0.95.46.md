# 0.95.46 — the build queue builds its own stand

## Fixed

- **A block whose only support is the block below could not be placed from the ground.** Its stand
  is beside the support, one level down; with no floor there and the cell itself not climbable (leaves
  above it), the build queue gave the cell back for ever. Found as a nether portal's top frame cell
  re-queued for ten minutes. The queue now walks to a neighbouring column, pillars up with a throwaway
  to the stand's level and places from there.

## Validation

- Stand probe (4-high column, leaves over the target, no side stand): placed in about 6 s.
- nav 17/17; craft 23/23 (portal-free courses; escape_lava and escape_lava_pool included).

## Known

- The portal builder re-sites its pad several times on rough ground; a resumed checkpoint forgets
  the portal it came through.
