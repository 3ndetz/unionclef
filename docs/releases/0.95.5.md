# 0.95.5 — dig up for air instead of drowning

A patch on 0.95.4, from the second 60-minute run's deadliest moment: the bot drowned at y=0
mining diamonds and lost its whole base to the world spawn.

---

## The fix (G100)

Submerged in a flooded pocket capped by stone, the survival chain's air-seek (`GetToAirTask`)
looked only for the nearest **existing** breathable cell to swim to — found none reachable, did
nothing, and the bot drowned (a zombie finished it). A player breaks straight up and surfaces.

`GetToAirTask` now, while submerged, scans its own column from the head up: water is the way up,
the first solid over the water is the cap and gets **dug** (the same miner the reach goal uses),
and only bedrock or a sideways-only pocket falls back to the old lateral search. Break a cap, the
water rises into the opened cell, swim up, repeat — the bot climbs out a block at a time.

**HOW TO TEST:** `python deploy/runner/air_pocket_test.py` — a walled water column lidded with
two stone layers so plain swim-up cannot surface; under `@gamer` the bot digs both caps and
surfaces (on this build: dig=2, surfaced with 16/20 hp in 13.8s). Before the fix it drowned every
time.

A note the bench itself taught: the survival chain only ticks while AltoClef is running, so the
faithful test runs `@gamer` (the real drown-while-mining case), not an idle `@stop`ped bot — an
idle bench never fires the air-seek at all and misreads a drown-and-respawn as success. Both
traps are now guarded in the bench.

## Known, not fixed

- With an iron pickaxe the escape still dips air slightly negative before surfacing (hp cost ~4);
  in the real case the bot has a diamond pickaxe (faster). If a future run still cuts it close,
  the next lever is triggering the air-seek earlier than air < half.
