# 0.95.3 — out of the water, onto the bank

A patch on 0.95.2, cut after the second sixty-minute playthrough on it. That run reached iron
tools at 11 minutes, a bucket at 13, dug for diamonds inside a stronghold (the scanner saw the
end-portal frames at (1826,-13,370)), rebuilt itself from nothing after a death, and was the
first to enter the nether stage of the game plan ("Going to Nether → Construct Nether Portal →
Getting flint & steel"). It also died three times and spent its last fifteen minutes at the lip
of a pool. The pool is fixed here; the deaths are recorded.

---

## The fix (two halves of one seam)

- **A swim stroke onto land arrives with the feet on the land, not by distance (G99).** The
  0.6-block arrival tolerance is for a body bobbing between two water cells. For the one-cell
  stroke out of the pool it declared SUCCESS with the feet still in the water, released the
  keys, the water carried the body back, and the navigator re-planned the same stroke from the
  same cell — 106 chains dropped, fifteen minutes.
- **A body in the water has not arrived at a goal on land (G99b).** Found by the new bench on
  its first run: the navigator's sphere arrival accepted a body in the water as settled,
  stopped the queue mid-stroke, and the body floated with no inputs until it drowned. Water
  counts as settled only when the goal itself is in water.

**HOW TO TEST:** `python deploy/runner/pool_bank_test.py` (a 3x3 pool two deep, the goal the
bank cell beside it, the bot starting on the pool floor): PASS is the feet on the bank — on
this build 2.3 s, twice; before the fix the bot drowned in it. `run_suite.py nav --only
nav_water` passes twice; the nav regression suite on this build: **14 of 14** (nav_gaps' known
first-go fall, passed on the retry).
