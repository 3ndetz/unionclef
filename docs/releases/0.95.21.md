# 0.95.21 — the flood-gather approaches FLOODABLE lava, not the nearest buried pocket (natural terrain)

Follow-up to 0.95.20. Removes the last stall the natural-terrain portal path hit before mining
obsidian: the flood task committing to a deep, unfloodable lava pocket it could not reach.

## The stall

`PlaceObsidianFloodTask`, when no floodable surface rim was directly reachable, fell back to
"approach the nearest lava" — `getNearestBlock(Blocks.LAVA)` of ANY kind. On natural terrain the
nearest lava is routinely a DEEP BURIED pocket. Measured on the `nether-reach` checkpoint: the bot at
the surface (y=61) committed to lava at y=27 directly below it, which

- cannot be flooded at all (no air above the source to land the water on), and
- `canReach()` reported reachable for the cell above it while the nav could not actually shaft ~34
  blocks down to it,

so it sat "Approaching lava to flood" for the whole window and never made obsidian.

## The fix

The fallback now approaches the nearest FLOODABLE lava — a SURFACE source (a source with air above
it) — so getting closer actually lets `findFloodRim` succeed on arrival; with none known it explores
for a lake instead of committing to an unreachable, unfloodable pocket. This only changes the fallback
taken when no rim is directly reachable (the natural-terrain case); the flat bench, where
`findFloodRim` succeeds immediately, is untouched.

|---|---|---|
| "Approaching lava to flood" stall ticks | ~9 min stuck | **0** |

`GAMER_SMOKE: PASS`, nether rung at 156 s. With the fix the bot chains the whole natural-terrain
pre-nether path: food buffer (0.95.20) -> flint -> obsidian rung -> "Building nether portal with
obsidian" -> flood the lava -> mine the obsidian. It did not finish lighting the portal inside the
14-minute bench window because the natural-terrain gather plus underground mob combat (skeletons,
creepers) is slower than the flat bench; the frame build itself is validated on the bench (isolated
8/8, full flood 6/6, v0.95.19).

## Known next frontier

The portal path is now gated only by TIME on natural terrain (deep obsidian mining + mob combat
inside one bench window), not by a stall. The next pass is either a longer window / a checkpoint
closer to the flood, or reducing the pre-nether gather cost. The build, flood, food and frame layers
are all fixed.
