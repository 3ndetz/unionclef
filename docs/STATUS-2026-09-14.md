## The goal this work serves

The `@gamer` bot (unionclef = altoclef + tungsten, MC 1.21.11) must play a survival start like a
player: gather wood, craft, get stone and iron, eat, survive the night — without standing still.
Every stand is treated as a defect with a root cause, a deterministic bench under
`deploy/runner/`, and a paragraph in `BARITONE-GAPS.md`. The measuring stick is the recorded
ten-minute playthrough on the survival stand (`uc_run.sh 10`): the item ladder, deaths, and the
longest stand.
