Unionclef 0.95.58: a stop now actually stops.

An explicit stop (stopPathing / ;stop) now abandons the queued mining or placing job and releases keys and aim. Before: the bot kept hitting obsidian for ~9 s after one stop. Now: 6/6 stops end the swing, the block stays intact; 42/42 adjacent courses still pass.

Near goals (since 0.95.56) route to any safe cell inside the radius, so a point inside a wall no longer stalls the approach. 6/6 fresh lava pickups from the same save.

Not claimed: speed or FPS gain. The long full59 lava stall and the whole playthrough are still open.
