the main field. Preserve the game's colours and HUD; no LUT, blur, invented
gameplay, substitute screenshots or decorative code windows. Keep actual

## Layout and rhythm

timeline. Every nested clip uses scene-local timing. The framework owns all
video playback. Hold picture scale at1.00 to preserve every HUD edge;
never animate the timed scene's positioning or call video.play/currentTime.

installed comparison-split's single divider state and settle/hold envelope
