Unionclef 0.95.63: water, air and buckets.

This lands the water work another agent session (UNIONCLEF-HARD) built and bench-tested in early October and never committed; reviewed, rebuilt over 0.95.62 and re-tested here.
- Pillaring in water uses swimming input (the old input held crouch+jump and sank at a bank); nav_water_pillar is now in the suite.
- The air escape starts when the way out needs it (dig vs swim priced), and a live route to air is followed to its end.
- One water bucket is kept, only extras are emptied.
- The block scanner reads chunk copies, not the live world from its worker thread.
