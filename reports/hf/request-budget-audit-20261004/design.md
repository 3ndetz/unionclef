# Design

Concept: each search carries its own stock; the footage shows the adjacent
behaviour, while a clearly labelled query diagram shows the actual fixed race.

Use warm off-white #eeeede, dark olive #181c16, accent lime #c8df81 and muted
text #b7c0aa. Headlines Montserrat900; labels and body IBM Plex Mono400,
locally embedded. A flat field keeps the measured diagram readable. Footage
is preserved in framed16:9 panels with no filters or extra crop. Two edge
anchors carry the event and its source/rate; bars and labels guide the eye.
Use64px headlines,30px body,26px labels, minimum60px edge clearance.
No emojis, claims of general gameplay completion, decorative glow or music.
